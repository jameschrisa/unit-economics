Based on the numbers above. Benchmarks are common rules of thumb, not targets for your business.

    Which business models this covers

    Three models, each as a one-time sale, repeat purchases or a subscription: DTC from your own store, B2C through marketplaces and retailers, and B2B as purchase orders or SaaS contracts. That is nine combinations.

    Close relatives you can model with the same inputs:

    • Mobile or web app with in-app purchases. DTC with subscription on. Put the app store's 15 to 30% cut in platform fees.
    • Marketplace operator. B2C, with average order value set to your take per transaction, not the buyer's full basket.
    • Agency or professional services. B2B with repeat customers off for project work, or repeat purchase on for retainers.
    • Wholesale and B2B2C. B2B with repeat purchase on. Put distributor margin in partner fees.
    • Usage-based software. B2B subscription, with contract value set to average annual usage revenue.

    Not covered: fixed overhead such as rent and G&A salaries, taxes, and the time value of money.
